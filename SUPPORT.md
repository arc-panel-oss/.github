# Getting help with ArcPanel

- **Questions:** ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/arc-panel-oss/arcpanel/discussions). Search first; someone may have answered already.
- **Guides:** installation, configuration and troubleshooting are in the [documentation](https://arcpanel.org/docs).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open an issue with the issue form of the repository. Issues are not for questions.
- **Security issues:** follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md), never a public issue.
- **Paid support:** plans with private tickets and response times are described at [arcpanel.org/support](https://arcpanel.org/support).

Never post passwords, keys, IP addresses or customer data in a discussion or an issue.
